XRPL Commons Make Waves 2026|Project: Proof-of-Attendance / Event Attendance NFT (poap.live)
June 21 – September 21, 2026|Team: Team Mikky

1. Executive Summary

  • Validated a real XRPL use case in enterprise and community settings, not just a prototype.
  • Built from the XRPL Commons Community Ideas repository: a Proof-of-Attendance / Event Attendance NFT framework.
  • Shipped as poap.live: soulbound attendance badges, live on XRPL mainnet.
  • Coordinated three real events across sports, education and technology audiences.
  • Result: 805 badges verified on the XRPL ledger against 842 registrations (96%).

2. Motivation

  • Enterprise Users need custodial wallets.
  • WAVES to act as a learning exercise for XRPL Enterprise Adoption.
